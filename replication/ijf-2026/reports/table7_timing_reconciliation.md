# Table 7: the Ipopt and SCIP times

Table 7 (`tab:local_global_forecasts`) prints Ipopt times of 0.59 s (simulated MA(1)), 0.0018 s
(simulated ARMA(1,0,1)) and 0.0020 s (PJME ARMA(1,0,1)), and SCIP times of 69.2, 385.4 and
900.0 s; its caption says that SCIP values and times refer to the archived runs and that the
first Ipopt solve includes compilation, and Section 5.6 says the archived Ipopt PJME solve took
about two milliseconds. This note identifies what was timed and which records the printed values
come from.

## What is timed

`run_solver_global_value.jl` (archived script and its port, identical in this respect) builds the
JuMP model, then records `rt = @elapsed optimize!(m)`: the wall time of JuMP's `optimize!` call,
which copies the model into the solver and solves it. Model construction, the forecast and the
writing of the record are outside it. Each instance is solved once; the three Ipopt solves run in
one Julia process in the order MA(1), ARMA(1,0,1) sim, ARMA(1,0,1) PJME, so the first one also
pays the compilation of the Ipopt/MOI code path.
