# Provenance gaps: printed values that the table groups do not regenerate

Every printed value of the submitted manuscript has an archived source
(`results/manuscript/manuscript_values.csv`): 660 are regenerated by the table groups of
`./reproduce.sh tables` from archived per-series or per-instance records, and 174 are
**derived-only**, compared with an archived record that no table group rebuilds. This report
lists the 174 by the kind of record behind them, what is missing, and whether a new execution
could close the gap. No value is counted as reproduced unless a table group regenerates it.

The rows add up to 174. The reconstructed records and aggregates (the last five rows, 73 values)
are the weakest evidence in the package: they agree with what survives of runs whose individual
outputs are lost. `certification_batch_2026_08.md` describes those records and how each value
follows from them.

## Values compared only at run time

Two values of Section 5.3 (the shares of the sum of absolute paired differences carried by the
top 5% of series, 41-78% across the 12 panels) are computed by `./reproduce.sh tables` from the
per-series records and have no archived reference value; they are compared only when the table
groups run.
