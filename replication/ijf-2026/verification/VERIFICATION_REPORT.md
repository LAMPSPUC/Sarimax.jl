**How the 200-iteration-ceiling share is calculated** (Table S9, Section 5.5). Each record of
the 1,500-series comparison is one automatic model-selection search (one series). The runner
stores `iterMax = maximum(iters)` over the candidate models that search fitted, and the share
is `100 * (number of searches with iterMax >= 200) / 1500`: the share of searches in which at
least one candidate reached the ceiling, neither a solver failure rate nor a share of
individual fits. `scripts/maintainers/check_gate_rerun_2026_10.py` recomputes it, and the mean
sMAPE and MASE, from the per-series records: 48.3% (`:penalized`) and 0.2% (`:innovations`),
as printed; the August aggregate printed beside them (47.4%, 0.2%) is the archived aggregate.
The provenance of every experiment, including this one, is in `reports/experiment_provenance.md`.

## 4. Mathematical consistency with the implementation

### 4.1 The moving-average normalization (Supplementary Section S2)

**What the supplement states.** With η_j the reflection coordinates of an MA polynomial,
D_reg(θ) = ∏_j [1 - η_j(θ)²]^(-j/T) for the regular polynomial (eq. app_d_reg), D_seas(Θ) =
∏_w [1 - η_w(Θ)²]^(-sw/T) for a pure seasonal one (eq. app_d_seas), and for the
multiplicative polynomial θ(B)Θ(B^s) the complete factor D_MA = D_reg D_seas [R(θ, Θ; s)²]^(-1/T),
R = Res(P_θ, Q_Θ) = ∏_i Θ(α_i^s) (eq. app_d_ma_complete); for the additive specification the
factor of the complete polynomial 1 + Σθ_jB^j + ΣΘ_wB^(sw). The concentrated criterion is
S_innov · D_MA.

**What the package does.** `src/models/sarima.jl`, in the `mse` objective under `:penalized` or
`:innovations` (`objectiveFunction == "mse" && penalizado`): the MA block multiplies the sum of
squares by ∏_j (1 - κ_j²)^(-j/T) for the regular MA and ∏_w (1 - κS_w²)^(-s·w/T) for the
seasonal MA, κ obtained from θ and Θ separately, whatever the seasonal specification. No
revision computes the resultant factor: the factor has this form at `14dd581` (v1.0.0, the
controlled objective comparison), at `aa68d57` and `3d7f883` (the six-frequency campaign) and at
`21903b5` (the last change to `src/` on this branch). Under `:penalized` with `stationary = true` the
same objective also carries the AR prior weights and the AR factor ∏_j (1 - κAR_j²)^(-j/T);
under `:innovations` it does not (the pre-sample AR values are generated), which is the case the
supplement describes.

**Checks.**

* `verification/ma_normalization/check_ma_normalization.py` (numpy; random invertible
  coefficients, s in {4, 12}, q and Q in {1, 2}): the reflection form equals the limit of the MA
  covariance determinant computed directly (largest log difference 3.3e-13), and the complete
  multiplicative factor equals D_reg D_seas R^-2 (4.3e-14): **the supplement's identity is
  exact**.
* `verification/ma_normalization/package_factor.jl` fits seasonal MA models (s = 4, T = 120) with
  fixed coefficients under `:innovations`, solves the inner minimum over the pre-sample
  innovations without the package, and divides the recorded objective by it. At `14dd581` and at
  `21903b5` the factor applied equals D_reg D_seas to eight decimals in all six cases (three
  multiplicative, three additive) and differs from the complete factor
  (`result_14dd581.txt`, `result_21903b5.txt`; 12/12 tests).

**Discrepancy.** The objective the package minimizes omits [R²]^(-1/T) for multiplicative
models with both regular and seasonal MA terms, and uses separate factors instead of the
complete polynomial's for additive ones. At T = 200 the omitted factor lies between 0.987 and
1.010 (multiplicative) and between 0.994 and 1.039 (additive) over random invertible draws; for
q = Q = 1, s = 12, θ₁ = 0.8, Θ₁ = -0.6 it is 1.00042. It affects fits with the `mse` objective
under `:penalized` or `:innovations` whose model has both regular and seasonal MA terms, and,
through the warm start, possibly the Huber fits started from them. The printed results are those of the
implementation as it is (they are regenerated from its records, section 2); what differs is the
description of the estimator. The authors record it as a known defect of the implementation:
neither the text nor the estimator is changed in this release, and no experiment was re-run
(`LIMITATIONS.md`, item 22).

### 4.2 Other settings stated by the text

Checked against the source at `14dd581` (claims C2, C7): the Huber threshold δ = 1.345 is
applied to `yValues ./ std(differenced series)`; the Huber fit is warm-started from the MSE fit
when no warm start is given; the ridge objective uses λ = sqrt(max(n_eff, 1)) and carries no
determinant factor; AR stationarity uses reflection coordinates with margin 1e-6
(`DEFAULT_DOMAIN_MARGIN`); the CVaR level is asserted in (0, 1). All agree with the text.

## 5. Targeted defect tests

`verification/defect_tests/` pins three properties of the automatic order search at a given
revision (each correct behaviour is a `@test_broken`, the value computed is pinned by a
`@test`). At `14dd581` (Julia 1.10.12): 13 pass, 3 broken, so each property reproduces:

* the stepwise search does not move from its incumbent when the first candidate is
  inadmissible and the null model is adopted (property C1 of `LIMITATIONS.md`, item 17);
* the ranking likelihood of a drift model with AR terms omits the level the drift implies
  (property C2 there);
* the ranking likelihood of a model with regressors under `:armax` evaluates the
  regression-with-errors model (fixed after `14dd581`: `src/exact_likelihood.jl`,
  `test/exog_likelihood.jl`).

The text states the first two as properties of the release used by the controlled comparison
(claim C5). (The labels C1 and C2 of `LIMITATIONS.md` are not the textual claims C1 and C2.)

## 8. Decisions and open items

Decided by the authors:

1. **Supplementary Section S2 (C8, C9):** a known defect of the implementation, documented in
   `LIMITATIONS.md` (item 22); neither the text nor the estimator is changed in this release.
2. **Table S3 (C3):** the over-differencing requirement of the 120-second experiment is added
   to the table.
3. **Independent runs cited by the text (C18-C20):** not deposited; the three sentences are
   restated to what the archived records support.
4. **Software version:** registering the `src/` fixes as 1.0.1 is on hold; the replication
   release identifies its code by commit.

5. **SCIP 8.0.0 (ZIB Academic License):** the image does not contain its binaries; the
   replication script downloads them on the user's machine, verified by content hash, on first
   use (`reports/scip_licence.md`). The image itself can therefore be published.

Items 2 and 3 change the manuscript: once the revised files exist, the mapping is transcribed
again from their LaTeX source, `REVISION.toml` records their SHA-256, and claims C3 and C18-C20
are checked again.
