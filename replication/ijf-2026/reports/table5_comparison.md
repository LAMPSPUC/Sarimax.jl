## Where the differences come from

Series by series (`derived/m4-objectives-controlled-2026-10/historical_vs_controlled.csv` and
`guard_domain_decomposition.csv`, bundle part 2):

* **MSE and MAE:** every change comes from series whose selected order changed, and all of them
  have `d + D >= 2`, the domain of the over-differencing guard that the historical campaigns had
  on (e.g. monthly MSE: 49 of 48,000 series change order; weekly MSE: 26 of 359, +0.0428 OWA).
* **Huber and Ridge:** the guard plus estimator changes between the feature-branch commits and
  v1.0.0 (Huber's warm-start scale; Ridge's determinant factor), which also move series outside
  the guard's domain (monthly Ridge: 12,281 series change order).

The historical and controlled campaigns must not be combined in one table.
