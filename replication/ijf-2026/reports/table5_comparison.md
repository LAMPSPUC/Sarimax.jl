# Table 5: the official values

Table 5 (`tab:m4_objectives`): OWA (relative to Naive2) of SARIMAX.jl under each estimation
objective, `initialization = :innovations`, against `forecast::auto.arima`, on M4 monthly,
quarterly, yearly and weekly.

Every number below is an official result of an archived run, regenerated by this package from
its raw records; nothing is mixed across runs.

* **MSE, Huber, MAE, Ridge and the Reference:** the controlled campaign `m4obj-6957c71f60f1`
  (release v1.0.0, one frozen protocol, every series under every objective, 381,436 fits, none
  failed), regenerated by the table group `m4_objectives_controlled` (760/760 cells agree with
  the campaign's independent scorer).
* **Stable (CVaR, alpha = 0.5):** the stable campaigns of August-September 2026, regenerated by
  `stable_admissibility_gavea` from the raw archive (1,028/1,028 cells). Each stable arm was run
  PAIRED with an MSE arm on the same series, commit and configuration, so the stable result is
  reported as that paired change in OWA. Its absolute OWA belongs to another run and is not put
  in the columns to its left.

## Coherence with Table 3

Table 3's MSE cells (campaign A: stationarity imposed by the reflection parameterization) and
Table 5's MSE column (controlled protocol: roots checked after estimation, as Section 5.1 says)
are two declared configurations: weekly 0.8977 against 0.9002, and at most 0.0004 apart in the
other three frequencies. The controlled campaign settles it: under the release and the same
environment, the Table 3 configuration reproduces 0.8977 on 359/359 weekly series, and the
Section 5.1 configuration gives 0.9002. One caption sentence states it (proposal in
`derived/m4-objectives-controlled-2026-10/manuscript_update_proposal.md` §4).

## What the table says (for the text)

1. MSE beats auto.arima in monthly (0.9039 vs 0.9044) and weekly (0.9002 vs 0.9266).
2. MAE is the best objective in quarterly (0.8955), and Ridge in yearly (0.8873); both beat
   auto.arima there.
3. Huber never beats auto.arima; MAE and Huber lose heavily in weekly.
4. Stable at alpha = 0.5 helps only in yearly (−0.0084, interval excludes zero), worsens weekly
   OWA (+0.0401, interval includes zero) and is neutral elsewhere: its value depends on the
   frequency.

## Superseded values (2026-10-16 revision)

The 2026-10-16 revision printed the historical objective campaigns of August 2026
(feature-branch commits, mixed commits inside the monthly cell, over-differencing guard on).
They are replaced by the values above; for the record, the largest changes are weekly MSE
0.9429 -> 0.9002, weekly Ridge 0.9721 -> 0.9436, monthly Ridge 0.9028 -> 0.9048, yearly Huber

estimator fixes made before v1.0.0 (`historical_vs_controlled.csv`, `guard_domain_decomposition.csv`,
bundle part 2).
